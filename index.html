Volmem

Set each app's volume once. It's there every time.

Audiobooks at 40. Music at 70. That one game at 20. Volmem puts each app's volume back whenever that app plays or comes to the screen. The app that's playing keeps its volume while you use other apps.

How it works

Add your apps. Set them once.

  1. Turn it on.

    Flip "Enable Volmem Control". Volmem takes you straight to the two Android settings it needs, then turns on by itself.

  2. Tap + and pick your apps.

    Each app gets a slider. Set it there, or use the volume keys while the app is playing.

  3. That's it.

    Whenever an app on your list plays or opens, its volume comes back. Everything else keeps your normal volume.

What it can and cannot do

A memory, not a mixer. Android has one media volume for the whole phone. Volmem switches it for you. It can't play two apps at different volumes at the same time.

  • Media volume only. Ring, notification, alarm and call volume are never touched.
  • The volume switches about a second after the app changes.
  • Nothing changes while the phone is locked.
  • After a restart, one tap turns Volmem back on. Android requires that.
  • Apps that don't report what they play (most games, videos in a social feed) follow the app on screen.
  • Some TV boxes and car units have fixed volume. Volmem can't change it there.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can Spotify play quietly while my game is loud?

Not at the same moment. Stock Android has one media volume. But if Spotify is the app playing, it keeps its volume while you use other apps. When a game or video takes over the sound, that app's volume comes in.

Why does it need Usage access?

To see which app is on screen. It's the only way to do that without an accessibility service.

Why does it need Notification access?

Android tells only apps with Notification access which app is playing. Volmem asks Android for no notifications at all and never reads them.

Why is there a notification?

Android requires one for anything that keeps working while you use other apps. It's silent and minimized, and you can hide it in Volmem's settings.

Will it drain the battery?

It checks about once a second, only while the screen is on and unlocked, and does nothing while the phone is locked. It doesn't read the screen, record audio or use the internet.

Does anything leave my phone?

No. Volmem has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere.
