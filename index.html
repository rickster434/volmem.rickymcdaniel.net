What Volmem can and cannot do

Volume memory (Volmem), not volume mixing. Android has one media volume control for the whole phone. Volmem's job is to adjust that media volume automatically for the apps you set. It cannot give two apps different volumes while they play at the same time.

  • Media volume only. Ring, notification, alarm and call volume are never touched.
  • The volume switches within about a third of a second after you change apps.
  • While the phone is locked or the screen is off, the volume stays where you left it. If a different app starts playing while the phone is locked, Volmem switches its volume when you unlock.
  • Volmem turns itself back on after your phone restarts.
  • Apps that don't report what they play (most games, videos in a social feed) follow the app on screen.
  • Some TV boxes and car units have fixed volume. Volmem can't change it there.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can Spotify play quietly while my game is loud?

Not at the same moment. Stock Android has one media volume. But if Spotify is the app playing, it keeps its volume while you use other apps. When a game or video takes over the sound, that app's volume comes in.

Why does it need Usage access?

To see which app is on screen. It's the only way to do that without an accessibility service.

Why does it need Notification access?

Android tells only apps with Notification access which app is playing. Volmem asks Android for no notifications at all and never reads them.

Why is there a notification?

Android requires one for anything that keeps working while you use other apps. It's silent and minimized, and you can hide it in Volmem's settings.

Will it drain the battery?

It checks which app is in front about three times a second, only while the screen is on and unlocked, and does nothing while the phone is locked. It doesn't read the screen, record audio or use the internet.

Does anything leave my phone?

No. Volmem has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere.
