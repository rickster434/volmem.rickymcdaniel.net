Volmem Sleep Timer & Alarm

Fall asleep to your book, music or rain sounds. They fade out once you're asleep.

Pick how long, tap Start and close your eyes. When the time is up, the sound slowly fades out and pauses, so your audiobook doesn't play all night and you don't lose your place. In the morning, an alarm can wake you through your earbuds, so no one else hears it.

How it works

Tap Start. Fall asleep. It turns itself off.

  1. Quick timer.

    Pick the minutes on the moon and tap Start. Whatever is playing fades out and pauses when the time is up.

  2. Routines.

    Save a timer, a sound and an alarm together, like "Weeknights" or "Nap". Start one with one tap.

  3. Starts by itself.

    A routine can start on its own when your book or music begins playing at bedtime.

What Volmem can and cannot do

  • The sleep timer pauses most audio and video apps. If an app ignores the pause, Volmem keeps it silent until you pick up your phone.
  • A music app alarm plays what that app played last. Android doesn't let one app choose a song inside another app at alarm time.
  • Your phone has one media volume. Volmem sets it for the app you're using; it can't play two apps at different volumes at the same time.
  • Light or dark, as you like, in Settings.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Will my audiobook lose its place?

No. Volmem pauses it, the same as pressing pause, so it picks up where you fell asleep.

Which apps does it work with?

Most apps that play audio or video: Audible, Spotify, YouTube, podcast apps and more. Volmem can also start your book or music for you when a routine starts.

Does the alarm ring if no headphones are in?

You choose, per alarm: ring on the phone instead, or stay quiet.

Will it drain the battery?

The sleep timer only runs while a timer is counting down (or during the bedtime hours you chose for Start by itself), then it stops. It doesn't read the screen, record audio or use the internet.

Why is there a notification?

Android requires one for anything that keeps working while you use other apps. The sleep timer's shows when it ends, with +15 min and Cancel.

Does anything leave my phone?

No. Volmem has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere.
